# Recommendation: continue the growth-first strategy, with conditions

**Question.** Is Duolingo's streak-driven daily habit still converting into revenue, and is its 2026 growth-over-monetization bet on track?

**Headline.** Duolingo's daily habit is getting stronger, but each user brings in less. The growth-first strategy only pays off in bookings if that turns around.

## Finding
Duolingo's daily habit is getting stronger: DAU/MAU rose from 28% to 42% since 2023, and users are growing faster than bookings. But bookings per daily user have fallen in 9 of 10 same-quarter comparisons. The base-case model shows growth-first trailing monetization-first by about $1.1 billion in total bookings by 2030. For growth-first to break even by 2030, bookings per user would have to grow about 4% a year after 2026. Today they are falling.

## What this means
On bookings alone, the growth bet does not pay back by 2030 unless money per user turns around. The case for the strategy therefore rests on value this data cannot measure, such as a larger user base and future monetization.

## Decision
Continue, and reassess if the warning lights worsen.

## Triggers to reassess
Each for two quarters in a row:
- DAU growth below 15%
- Bookings per DAU worse than -11.4% year over year
- Paid subscribers per DAU down more than 0.5 points year over year
- Adjusted EBITDA margin below 24.3%

## Current scorecard (Q2 2026)

## Limits
- Public company data only; 14 quarters (Q1 2023 to Q2 2026).
- 2027 to 2030 values are assumptions (see claims_and_assumptions_register.csv).
- Bookings per DAU is a proxy, not revenue per user.
- No causal claim about the streak: the streak evidence is company-reported and correlational.
- Thresholds for the scorecard are derived from only 10 quarters of year-over-year history.
